## Design system

| Area          | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Glyphs        | `TileGlyph`: operators drawn as SVG on one 24-unit grid (shared stroke, centre, size) for + − × ÷ =; digits as heavy tabular text. Last Move and Turn Log draw expressions with the same glyphs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Alternatives  | Unchosen +/− or ×/÷: both signs small and neutral. Chosen: the sign large in blue plus a small neutral mark of the other. Blank chosen: value in blue plus a small blank mark. Blue means only "played as".                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Choosing      | `FacePicker`: placing an alternative opens it at once — two large buttons for signs, every legal value (0–20, + − × ÷ =) for a blank. One tap; no default value; no cycling; no hold/double-tap gestures. Docked over rack/actions on phones, anchored to the tile on desktop. Light dismiss (an outside tap only closes it). Keyboard: E opens it for the tile under the cursor, arrows move, Enter chooses, Escape closes. |
| Tile states   | Committed: plain. Last move: small corner dot in the mover's colour after a brief flash. Tentative: thin amber edge + slight lift. Selected: blue edge. No flags, dashed frames or cell tints over the glyph.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Board         | Turn shown by a thin edge + soft glow outside the frame (no thick red box). Coordinates A–O / 1–15 in a thin band (10 px on phones, ≤ 18 px on desktop). Cell names are `H8`-style.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| HUD           | `Scoreboard`: both players in one table — name, lead (+N dark chip / −N muted / ±0), score, clock. Turn = side colour (tint, bar, running mark, bold clock); lead = neutral.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Unseen        | Always visible: compact strip (total, bag, digits/10–20/operators/=/blanks) on phones, tablets and short desktops; the full distribution grid in tall desktop gutters. Derived from the public board and own rack only.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Rack          | Selection never moves a tile (light inner ring); Exchange-marked tiles get a slate face, white glyph and a check. In Exchange mode a press dragged across the rack marks (or unmarks) every tile it crosses; tap and keyboard still toggle. Reorder by drag works outside Exchange.                                                                                                                                          |
| Mobile rhythm | HUD · board · Last Move · Unseen · rack · actions, with 8 px margins and 6 px gaps; rows share the board's width. Short phones drop the Last Move row (compact) or use a small two-row HUD with the Unseen total in the action bar (tight).                                                                                                                                                                                  |
